The small things that make a trip easier

Six ways to split

Equal, exact amounts, percentages, shares, plus or minus per person, or by item, with a clear breakdown for every expense.

Receipts

Take a picture or choose a file, and keep the receipt with the expense.

Comments and mentions

Ask about an expense right where it is, and mention the person who knows.

Works offline

No signal abroad? Add expenses anyway, and Splyto syncs them when you’re back online.

Notifications

Hear about new expenses, invites, mentions and payments. You choose what reaches your phone.

Trip activity

See what changed in a trip, and finish it when everyone is ready to settle.

16 languages

English, Latvian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Dutch, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Czech, Ukrainian and Russian.

Light and dark

Follow your phone’s appearance, or pick the look you prefer.

Easy sign-in

Use Apple, Google or your email address.

Ready in three steps

  1. Create a trip

    Choose solo or group, pick a currency and set a budget if you want one.

  2. Add expenses

    Everyone adds what they paid, in any currency, from their own phone.

  3. Settle up

    Finish the trip, see who pays whom and confirm when it’s done.

Your trips stay yours

No ads, no public feeds, and we don’t sell your personal data. What you share in a trip goes to the people on that trip. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.

  • Your travel map is visible only to you
  • Live location is off until you turn it on, and it stops when your chosen time ends
  • Payment details are shared only while a payment request is waiting, and only if you agree
  • You can delete your account from the app
