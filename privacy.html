Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Splyto helps you keep track of travel costs, alone or with the people you travel with. To do that, we have to store some information about you, and the people you share a trip with will see some of it. This policy explains what that information is, why we need it, who else is involved and what you can do about it. It covers the Splyto apps for iPhone and Android, the website at splyto.eu and the emails we send.

The short version

  • We use your data to run Splyto for you. We don’t sell it, and the app has no ads, no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics.
  • What you add to a trip is visible to the members of that trip. Your bank details stay private unless you choose to share them.
  • Your travel map is private. Live location is off until you start it, for a time you choose, and only the latest position is kept.
  • Our server is in Helsinki, Finland. A few providers, such as Apple, Google, Mapbox and our email service, also handle some data to deliver their part of the service.
  • You can see, correct, export or delete your data. Write to info@splyto.eu.

1. Who is responsible

Splyto, based in Latvia, is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. For anything related to privacy, write to info@splyto.eu. For general help with the app, write to support@splyto.eu.

2. What we collect

Your account

When you create an account we store your email address, the name or nickname you choose, and, if you add one, a profile photo. We also store your preferred currency, language and notification settings. If you set a password, we keep only a secure hash of it, never the password itself.

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an identifier for your account at that provider, plus the name and email address the provider shares with us. With Apple you can hide your real address and give us a relay address instead. We never see your Apple or Google password.

Trips and expenses

Splyto stores the trips you create or join, their members and invitations, and the dates, destinations and cover photo of each trip. For every expense we keep the description, amount, currency, date, category, who paid, who takes part and how the cost is split, along with any comments, reactions and receipt photos. We also store settlements, payment requests and an activity history, and we calculate balances, budgets and spending summaries from these records.

Some of this information may be about you even if you didn’t enter it yourself. For example, a friend can add an expense that you take part in, or comment on one. If you take part in a trip, we treat what other members record about you in it as part of the trip.

Friends

If you add friends, we store those connections, friend requests and the personal link or QR code that lets someone add you.

Payment details (optional)

To make it easier for friends to pay you back, you can add an account-holder name, IBAN and BIC/SWIFT, and Revolut, PayPal or Wise usernames or links. Splyto only displays these details. It doesn’t move money, and we never ask for card numbers or online-banking passwords.

Your travel map (optional)

The travel map stores the cities you mark as visited: the city, its country and region, the coordinates of the city centre, your visit dates, and an optional title, linked trip and photo. When you tap “I’ve been here”, your phone reads your location once and suggests the nearest city. We only receive the city you confirm, not your exact position. You can always search for a city by name instead.

Live location (optional)

In a group trip you can share your live location with the other members for 15 minutes, 1, 4 or 8 hours. Only while you are sharing do we receive your coordinates, their accuracy and the time they were taken. We keep only your latest position, not the route you travelled, and we remove it when sharing ends.

Photos

You can add a profile photo, trip covers, visit photos and receipts. We resize the photos, convert them to a web format and remove location and other hidden metadata. When you crop a photo, we also keep the uncropped version so you can change the crop later. That original has no public address and only you can open it. For a trip cover, the people allowed to edit the trip can open it too.

Devices and notifications

To send push notifications we store an identifier for your app installation, a delivery token, your platform and language, and the notifications you want. Inside the app, your phone keeps your settings, a copy of your trips so the app works offline, and changes waiting to sync. Your sign-in credentials are kept in the phone’s secure storage.

Technical data

When the app or website talks to our server, the server records technical information such as your IP address, the time, the device and app version, and which request was made. We use it to keep the service running, find errors and protect accounts against abuse.

Messages you send us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. Older app versions could also send feedback with screenshots and app details.

3. What other people can see

  • Other Splyto users can find you by name, nickname or email. Search results show your name, profile photo and an account identifier, but not your email address.
  • Trip members can see the trip and everything recorded in it, including the expenses, comments, receipts, balances and activity, and your name and profile photo.
  • Your payment details stay private by default. Someone can see them only if you attach them to a payment request you send to that person, or if you turn on “Share payment methods with my trips”. With that setting on, accepted members of your trips can see them. Being someone’s friend is not enough.
  • Your live location is visible only to accepted members of the trip you are sharing it with, and only while you share.
  • Your travel map is private to you. Joining a trip doesn’t share your visits.

Please keep in mind that we can’t take back something another person has already seen, copied or downloaded, and that a receipt link stays valid for a short time after it is created.

6. What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your personal data or share it with anyone for their marketing.
  • We don’t show ads, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics trackers.
  • We don’t read your receipts with AI or send them to outside services.
  • We don’t track your location in the background unless you have started live location sharing yourself.

8. Your choices and rights

In the app

  • Change your name, photo, settings and payment details in your profile. To change your email address, write to us.
  • Turn sharing of payment details with your trips on or off, stop live location at any time, and remove visits from your travel map.
  • Choose which notifications you get, in the app and in your phone’s settings.
  • Deactivate your account, or delete it in Profile → Account settings. Deletion is confirmed by email. It removes your profile, contact and payment details, friends, sign-in links and sessions. Your name in trips you shared with others is replaced, and the amounts stay so their balances remain correct.

Under the GDPR

You have the right to access your data and receive a copy, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict how we use it, to receive the data you gave us in a portable format, and to object to uses based on our legitimate interests. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting what was done before.

Write to info@splyto.eu. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We answer within one month. If a request is complex, we may need longer, and we will tell you within that first month. Some rights have limits. For example, we can’t delete expenses that are also part of another member’s records, but we can remove what identifies you in them where that is possible.

9. Your device and our website

The app asks for permission before it uses your camera (for photos and QR codes), your photo library (for photos you choose), your location (for the travel map or live location) and notifications. You can change these permissions at any time in your phone’s settings.

Our website doesn’t use advertising or analytics cookies and doesn’t track you. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the site from automated attacks.

10. Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, sign-in tokens are kept in your phone’s secure storage, access to data is checked on every request, and administrative access requires two-factor authentication. We make daily backups. No system is completely secure, so please share only what a trip needs. If a security incident puts your data at risk, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Children

Splyto is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to info@splyto.eu and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Splyto changes the way it handles data, and the date at the top will change. If a change matters for you, for example a new kind of data or a new provider, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
