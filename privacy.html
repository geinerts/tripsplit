Splyto Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Splyto (package id com.tripsplit.app.tripsplit) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, nickname, email, encrypted password hash.
  • Trip data: trips, members, expenses, settlements, and related notes.
  • Uploaded content: receipt images, trip images, profile avatar.
  • Device/app information: app version, platform, basic diagnostics, push token for notifications.

Optional payment-method sharing

You can also enable “Share payment methods with my trips” in Profile → Payment methods. This is off by default. When enabled, accepted members of trips you create or join can view these details, including in current and future trips. Pending invitations and friendship alone do not grant access. Turning it off stops new access through trip member profiles; separately shared payment requests retain their own sharing rules. Information already seen or copied cannot be withdrawn.

Your private travel map

The optional “My travel map” stores cities you confirm, their country and region, city-centre coordinates and visit dates in your Splyto account. This map is private to your account; joining a trip does not share these visits with other participants. You can remove individual visits from their map markers. Confirmed account deletion removes the map records from the active database; routine backups follow the recovery window described below.

You can optionally associate a confirmed visit with a trip you have joined, add a personal title and choose a photo. Planned trip destinations are visible to that trip’s participants; they do not automatically become personal visits. When you save a visit from a trip, its name and available cover photo are copied into your private visit. That copy can remain after the trip is changed or deleted; a link back is available only while you still have access to the trip. You can replace or remove the private photo without changing the shared trip. Photos are resized, converted to WebP and stripped of metadata. They are available through authenticated requests for your account, cached locally with your account data, and removed with the visit or confirmed account deletion.

“I’ve been here” requests location access only when you tap it. Your phone uses a one-time location reading to suggest a city through its native geocoding service. Splyto receives the city centre, not that device location, only after you confirm the visit. Manual city search does not require location permission. Search terms and coordinates used to suggest a city may be processed by your platform’s geocoding provider, under its own terms. This feature does not track you in the background or share live locations.

The overview map is bundled in the app; cached visits can be displayed offline. Searching, resolving the current city and synchronising visits may require a connection. Local map records use account-scoped app storage and are cleared with sign-out cleanup.

Permissions

Splyto may request the following permissions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): used for QR code scanning and taking photos (for receipts/profile).
  • Photos/Media: used to select and upload images.
  • Notifications: used to deliver trip and payment updates.

How We Use Information

  • To provide trip expense splitting features.
  • To sync your data across devices and sessions.
  • To send notifications related to your trips.
  • To improve app reliability, performance, and security.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only as needed to operate the service (for example, hosting, database, and push delivery providers), and only for service functionality.

Data Retention

We keep account and trip data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children

Splyto is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

  • You can update account details in the app profile settings.
  • You can manage app permissions in your device settings.
  • You can stop using the app at any time.
