1. What Splyto is

Splyto is a tool for keeping track of travel expenses, on your own or with other people. You can create trips, add expenses, split them in different ways, convert currencies, set a budget, see who owes whom, mark debts as settled and keep a private map of the places you have visited.

Splyto is a record-keeping tool. It is not a bank, payment service, accountant or tax adviser, and it doesn’t receive, hold or transfer money for anyone.

3. Trips and shared records

A trip is a shared space. Its members can see what is recorded in it, and depending on their role they can add, edit or delete expenses, comments and photos. Whoever creates a trip can manage its details and members.

  • Only invite people you actually travel or share costs with, and only add them to expenses they take part in.
  • Records in a trip belong to the whole group. If you leave a trip or delete your account, what you added can stay in the trip so the others keep a correct history.
  • We don’t check whether expenses are accurate or fair. If you disagree with something in a trip, sort it out with the other members.

4. Money, balances and payment links

  • Balances are informal. The amounts Splyto shows are calculated from what you and your fellow travellers enter. They are not a demand for payment from Splyto, and we are not a party to any arrangement between you and other users.
  • Check before you pay. Calculations depend on the data entered, and rounding can make a cent or two of difference. Confirm the amount with the other person before you send money.
  • Exchange rates are for reference. Currency conversions use published daily rates, which can differ from what your bank or card charged.
  • Payments happen elsewhere. If someone shares bank details or a Revolut, PayPal or Wise link, the payment is made through that bank or service under its terms. We are not responsible for those services or for payments made through them. Marking a debt as settled in Splyto doesn’t move any money.
  • Never use Splyto to collect money from people you don’t know, or for anything connected with fraud or money laundering.
